Independent Researcher

2025 – 2026 · Senior year
AP Research (AP Capstone) · University High School

Enhancing Phishing Website Detection with Machine Learning

Studied how the size of the training set affects machine learning classifiers that detect phishing URLs. Converted about 450,000 labeled URLs from the Mendeley dataset into BERT embeddings, then trained logistic regression models on samples ranging from 1,000 URLs to the full dataset, with five random seeds per size.

F1 rose from 0.978 at 1,000 URLs to 0.996 on the full set, and results became more consistent as the data grew. ANOVA and post-hoc tests showed the gains level off: models trained on 100,000 URLs or more were not significantly different from each other, which points to a saturation point where more data adds little.
